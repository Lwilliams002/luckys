Our Story

Visit our Miami matcha & espresso bar.

Made with care

Two traditions, one roof

Lucky’s was born from a simple belief: that your daily drink should be made with care. We opened our espresso & matcha bar in North Miami Beach to bring two of the world’s great café traditions under one roof — the bold, concentrated craft of Italian espresso and the meditative ritual of Japanese matcha.

Nothing rushed

Sourced, roasted and whisked with intention

Every bean is sourced and roasted with intention. Every scoop of matcha is stone-milled, ceremonial grade, whisked the way it has always been done. Nothing is rushed. Nothing is cut short.

Named for the maneki-neko — the beckoning cat, a quiet symbol of welcome and good fortune — Lucky’s is a place to slow down, sip well, and feel at home. Come find us on Biscayne Blvd in North Miami Beach. We’d love to have you.
